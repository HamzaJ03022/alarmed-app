Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Overview

Alarmed is an alarm clock application that helps you wake up by requiring you to answer questions or type a phrase to dismiss alarms. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

Data We Collect

Alarmed stores all your app data — alarms, settings, history, and quotes — locally on your device. We do not operate our own servers that receive your alarms or history.

Alarmed is a paid app offered through auto-renewing subscriptions. To process purchases and subscriptions we use Apple's in-app purchase system and RevenueCat as our subscription management provider:

  • Apple processes the payment. Apple handles your payment details; we never see them.
  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and your transaction receipts to validate purchases, manage the subscription entitlement, and handle restores. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

We do not use your subscription data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

Permissions

Alarmed requests the following permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • Notifications: to schedule and deliver alarm notifications at your specified times.
  • Alarms: to ring system alarms that display over the lock screen.
  • Audio: to play alarm sounds when an alarm is triggered.
  • Vibration: to vibrate your device when an alarm rings.

Third-Party Services

Apart from Apple (payments) and RevenueCat (subscription validation) described above, Alarmed does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Alarmed is suitable for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the app and on this page.

Common questions

My alarm didn't ring. What should I check?

Open Settings on your iPhone, find Alarmed, and make sure Alarms and Notifications are allowed. Alarmed uses the system alarm, so it rings in Silent and Focus modes once permission is granted.

How do I stop an alarm?

Each alarm has a wake-up challenge: answer the questions or type the phrase exactly as shown. Capital letters and extra spaces don't matter, but punctuation does. Wrong characters are marked in red so you can fix them.

What is the wake-up check-in?

A few minutes after you dismiss an alarm, Alarmed asks if you're still awake. If you don't answer, the alarm rings again.

How do I delete an alarm?

Swipe left on the alarm in your list and tap Delete. You can also switch an alarm off with its toggle.

How do subscriptions work?

Alarmed Pro is available weekly, monthly or yearly and renews automatically until cancelled. Manage or cancel it any time in your iPhone's Settings under your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged again.

I changed phones. How do I get Pro back?

Open the subscription screen in Alarmed and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.
